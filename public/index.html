Reading a result

Each answer uses a different shape and pattern, so you never have to rely on colour alone.

  • Looks fine

    Round with a tick. The rate is fair or no warning signs were found.

  • Check first

    Triangle with a dotted edge. Something isn't quite right, so pause before paying.

  • Stop

    Striped octagon with a cross. A rip-off rate or clear scam signs. Don't pay.

payprooof is a quick safety check, not financial advice. Exchange rates are mid-market rates from Frankfurter.
